How to copy a trade on Binance

  1. When a "Decided to buy" step appears, buy the coin on Binance (spot, USDT pair) with the same share of your account, using a market order.
  2. When the "Bought" step appears (about an hour later), place one OCO order to sell everything: the take profit as the limit price and the safety exit as the stop price. Both prices are in that step and under "Trades open now".
  3. If neither is hit by the sell-by time, cancel the OCO order and sell at the market price.
  4. Your prices, fees and timing will never match exactly, so your result will differ a little from this page. Only use money you can afford to lose: this is a paper test, not advice, and it has no proven record yet.

The rules it follows

  • Capital first. Staying in cash is always allowed and is the default. It only buys a Small coins Day idea (sell within 24 hours) graded Moderate or better that is still expected to make money after every cost.
  • No trades while the Small coins grade check says grades can't be trusted, or while the Day speed is on probation: when its last 50 ideas did no better than picking coins at random (judged on Small coins' own record).
  • At most 25% of the account in one trade, sized so that hitting the safety exit loses at most 2% of the account.
  • At most 2 trades open and 1 new trade an hour.
  • No new trades for the rest of the day after a 5% loss in a day (UTC). Everything pauses if the account falls 20% below its peak, until the owner restarts it.

Why the results are realistic

  • No bots, no head start. It acts on the same hourly list anyone can see, and buys at the price at the end of the hour the list came out.
  • Every cost: Binance's 0.1% fee each way, the gap between the buy and sell prices (read from the live order book when it decides), and slippage on market and stop orders.
  • Real exchange orders. The take profit is a limit order: it fills at its price once the price trades above it. The safety exit is a stop order: it fills at its price minus the gap and slippage, or at the opening price if the price jumped straight past it. If both are touched in the same hour, it assumes the safety exit.
