Stealth accumulation: the top 600 coins

The pattern: a coin goes quiet; someone buys it in small chunks over many days (selling a little now and then as cover) until they hold a meaningful share; liquidity is added, the price is pumped, and they sell into it. This scans the 600 largest coins for that quiet buying, buys on paper when it appears, and sells on the pump (+60%), on the safety exit (−30%), when the accumulators start selling, or after 90 days. Ethereum coins are replayed over their last 2 years first, so we can see how this would have done before trusting it.

How it works, and what it can't see

  • Five kinds of evidence, never mixed. A a DEX swap whose signing wallet bought or sold the token (verified on-chain). B a wallet's balance changed (not proof of a purchase). C exchange buying pressure (no buyer identities). D transfers, never counted as buying. E links between wallets: not inferred at all, so every number here counts addresses, not people.
  • Three ways onto the list, each with its own rule: A verified net buying of 0.1%+ of the circulating supply in 7 days ($25k+, buying on 4+ days, swaps seen 80%+ of the time); B one address net buying 0.05%+ of supply in 14 days and at least doubling what it held (or a new holder); C 15+ addresses net buying against fewer sellers. Separately, exchange flow: Binance coins where market buyers took a larger share of volume for 14 days while the price stayed quiet.
  • Round trips are removed: a wallet buying and selling nearly the same amount in a day (arbitrage and market-making bots) isn't counted. A wallet buying many small chunks is counted: that is what quiet accumulation looks like.
  • The score (0-100) is a research ranking, not a probability. Each part shows its numbers; missing data earns nothing. Confidence is separate: High, Medium or Low with stated criteria.
  • What the free data can't see: on Solana the free feed gives only the last 300 swaps per pool, so very busy pools have gaps (shown) and Solana history starts the day this lab started; top-holder lists aren't available for free; Ethereum balances and transfers need the free Etherscan key; no free source gives token unlock dates or project catalysts, so those show as "not measured".
  • Honest testing. Every detection is judged 7, 14, 30, 60 and 90 days later against the other tokens watched over the same days. The recovery strategy is backtested on every Binance coin, delisted ones included. Both stay Testing only (never in alerts or the home page's best idea) until they pass.
  • Stealth accumulation rules (fixed before any result): over the last 60 days an address bought on 5+ separate days, in small chunks (median buy $5,000 or less), sold no more than 30% of it, and kept at least 0.05% of the circulating supply; together these addresses hold 0.5%+ of the supply; the price moved less than 30% either way over 30 days (not pumping yet). Addresses first funded by the same wallet are grouped as probably one owner (never exchanges or services). Adding or removing liquidity is recorded separately, never as buying or selling. Only data up to each day is used: no hindsight.
  • Paper only. The $100 account never trades real money, no wallet or key that can trade exists, and a watchlist entry never opens a trade by itself.
