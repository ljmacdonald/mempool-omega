How to read this

  • Every result is after costs (fees, spreads, slippage and, on DEXes, bots and network fees), on paper, and recorded automatically as it happens. Nothing is removed afterwards.
  • Random picks are the honest yardstick: the same rules applied to coins or markets chosen at random over the same hours. Beating zero is not enough; a rising market lifts everything.
  • Ahead made money and beat random picks by a clear margin. Even made money but no more than random picks. Behind lost money. Too early fewer than 30 finished trades: nothing can be said yet.
  • "Ahead" is not proof: a section can be ahead by luck over a few days. Each page carries stricter checks (probation, the grade check, tests on data the models never saw), and its own Track record.
Mempool Omega is research and education software. Everything here is paper trading: no real orders. Past results do not predict future results. Not financial advice.
