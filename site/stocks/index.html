What this page does

While the US market is open, the system checks two lists every 15 minutes:

  • Large stocks: the 200 most-traded US companies worth $10 billion or more. This covers most of the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 by trading activity.
  • High volatility: companies worth $300 million to $10 billion, priced above $3 and trading at least $10 million a day, that rose 20% or more within a week at least 15% of the time over the last year. They can move 20–50%, and they fall just as fast.

A model trained every night on recent price history estimates the chance each stock does better than the others over your chosen time. That becomes a score after costs, with a take-profit, a safety exit and a sell-by time, exactly like the crypto pages.

The two speeds

  • Today: sell within 2 hours, and always before the 4 pm close. Ideas aren't held overnight.
  • Few days: hold for up to 3 trading days. Prices can jump overnight, and results announcements ("earnings") inside that window get a heavy penalty and a warning.

Things to know about US stocks

  • Market hours: Monday to Friday, 9:30 am to 4:00 pm New York time. The banner at the top shows whether it's open and the time in New York.
  • Day-trading rule: US brokers limit margin accounts under $25,000 to 3 same-day buy-and-sell round trips in any 5 business days. Cash accounts avoid this, but your money from a sale takes a day to settle.
  • Costs: most brokers charge no commission. You still pay the gap between buying and selling prices (the spread), a tiny US regulatory fee when you sell and, if your account isn't in US dollars, a currency conversion fee each way. Pick your broker's rate in "Currency fee"; it's often the biggest cost.
  • Results days ("earnings"): a company's quarterly results can move its price 5–20% in a moment. The page shows the date when it's close.
  • Prices without a key are refreshed every 15 minutes while the market is open. Add a free Finnhub key above for live prices.

Honest limits

Stock prices are hard to predict: tested on past data, the model's edge is small. The Track record tab shows how its ideas actually turn out, so judge it by that, over weeks. Paper trading and education only; never use money you can't afford to lose.
