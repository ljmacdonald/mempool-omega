Trading can lose all the money you put in

Crypto, DEX tokens, stocks, forex, gold and prediction markets can fall fast, sometimes to zero. Small and brand-new coins are especially risky: in the site's own tests, 97% of brand-new coins that passed the first, looser safety checks were rug-pulled within 12 hours.

What paper results leave out

  • The site's results are simulated. Real trades can fill at worse prices, later, or not at all, and fees can differ.
  • The site counts fees, spreads, slippage and (on DEXes) bots and network fees, but your real costs may be higher.
  • A section being "ahead" can be luck over a short period. Most sections have not proven an edge: see the Results page.

Leverage, shorting and stops

  • Leverage multiplies losses as well as gains and can wipe out an account.
  • Stop orders can fill far below their price when prices jump, and decentralised exchanges have no stop orders at all.

Scams and technical risks

  • Tokens can be honeypots (you can buy but not sell), have hidden taxes, or have their pool money pulled. The site's checks reduce but cannot remove these risks.
  • Exchanges, wallets and blockchains can fail, freeze withdrawals or be hacked.
