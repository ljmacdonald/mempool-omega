The short version

The site has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. What you set up on the site (your followed trades, settings and chat history) is stored only in your own browser, on your device. We never receive it.

What stays in your browser

  • Trades you follow on each page, alert settings and alerts already shown, Ask Omega chat history (last 30), tour progress and hidden tips, display and page settings, and a Finnhub key if you enter one on the US stocks page.
  • Clearing your browser data removes all of it. You can move your trades between devices with "Move my trades to another device".

Who your browser talks to

To show live prices and checks, your browser connects directly to public services, which see your IP address under their own privacy policies: GitHub (site hosting and data files), Google Fonts, Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Gate.io, Bitget, HTX, GeckoTerminal, DEX Screener, GoPlus, Honeypot.is, RugCheck, Hyperliquid, public blockchain nodes (PublicNode), Finnhub (only with your own key) and unpkg (a code library).

Data we hold

None about you personally. The site's own data (prices, ideas, track records, paper accounts) is published openly. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep server logs under its own privacy statement.
