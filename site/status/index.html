How this works

  • Every 15 minutes a watchdog checks when each page last updated and whether any background job has failed twice in a row.
  • If something is wrong, the affected page shows a warning at the top, and the owner is notified automatically. It clears itself when things are back to normal.
  • US stocks and forex only update while their markets are open, so old data at weekends and overnight is expected, not a fault.
