How this report is made

  • At the start of every week (Monday 00:00 UTC) a copy of every section's published record is frozen. The difference between two copies is that week: the trades that finished in it and their average after every cost.
  • Random picks are the same rules applied to randomly chosen coins or markets over the same week. A week that made money but did worse than random picks was probably just a rising market.
  • The figures come only from each page's own published record, so they can't disagree with the pages. If a page's record was cleaned up during a week, that week's figure for it is left blank rather than guessed.
  • One week proves nothing either way: a few good or bad trades can swing it. The status column is each section's whole record so far, the same as the Results page.
Mempool Omega is research and education software. Everything here is paper trading: no real orders. Past results do not predict future results. Not financial advice.
