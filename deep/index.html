How it works, and what it can't see

  • The idea: a few coins that lose 92–98% come back several times over, usually after a real change (buybacks or burns start, emissions are cut, a big unlock passes). Engine B looks for that change, measured, never guessed.
  • Catalysts are detected from data, never typed in: holders revenue from DefiLlama (buybacks, burns, staker share) and DefiLlama's open token-unlock schedules, each from the day the data first showed it. Product relaunches and settled legal or hack issues have no reliable free source, so they are not counted.
  • What history can't include: catalysts, fees, unlocks and developer activity per day are not available for every past coin, so the history test and backtest use the price rules only (the full rules are stricter).
  • Rules fixed first. Every threshold is the specification's default (deep/profile.yaml); none is tuned on these results.
  • All free, nothing manual: Binance and Coinbase public price data (delisted coins included), FRED, DefiLlama.
  • Paper only. No real orders, no wallet, no key that can trade. A halted engine opens nothing, not even on paper.
